🔌 What is a circuit?

A circuit is a path that electricity travels around. It has to be a complete loop — like a racetrack with no holes in it!

  1. A battery pushes the electricity. 🔋
  2. Wires carry it around the loop. 〰️
  3. A bulb lights up when the electricity gets there. 💡
  4. A switch can stop the electricity or let it go. 🎚️

👉 If there is even one gap in the loop, the electricity stops and the bulb goes dark.

🌟 Your turn to discover!

Try the static electricity trick: rub a balloon on your hair for 10 seconds, then hold it near tiny paper bits. The paper jumps up! That's electricity you can see — and it's the very same thing the ancient Greeks noticed thousands of years ago.
